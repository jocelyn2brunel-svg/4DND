## Terrain Type Effects

```
Forest ??
- Dense trees
- Limited visibility
- Abundant wildlife
- Good for rangers

Hills ???
- Elevated terrain
- Mines and caves
- Defensible positions
- Good for dwarves

Mountains ??
- Extreme elevation
- Dragon lairs
- Ancient ruins
- Harsh conditions

Plains ??
- Open grasslands
- Easy travel
- Farms and villages
- Cavalry advantage

Swamp ??
- Murky water
- Disease risk
- Hidden dangers
- Difficult terrain

Desert ???
- Extreme heat
- Limited water
- Ancient tombs
- Nomadic tribes

Coast ??
- Ocean access
- Port towns
- Naval combat
- Trade routes
```
