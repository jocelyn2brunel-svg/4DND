## Memory Aid: The 6-60 Rule

```
???????????????????????????????????????????????????????????
?              THE 6-60 RULE                              ?
???????????????????????????????????????????????????????????

1 PROVINCE hex = 1 mile
   ? ×6
1 KINGDOM hex = 6 miles
   ? ×10
1 CONTINENT hex = 60 miles

CONVERSION FACTORS:
  Province ? Kingdom:  ÷ 6
  Kingdom ? Continent: ÷ 10
  Province ? Continent: ÷ 60

EXAMPLE:
  Location at Province (60, 30)
  = Kingdom (10, 5)    [60÷6=10, 30÷6=5]
  = Continent (1, 0.5) ? (1, 0) [10÷10=1, 5÷10=0.5]
```
