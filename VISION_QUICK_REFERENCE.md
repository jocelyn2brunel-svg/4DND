# Vision System Quick Reference

## Light Levels
| Type | Effect | Color |
|------|--------|-------|
| Bright | Normal vision | White |
| Dim | Disadvantage on Perception | Gray |
| Darkness | Blinded (without darkvision) | Black |

## Vision Types by Range
| Type | Range | Special |
|------|-------|---------|
| Normal | 120 ft | Needs light |
| Darkvision | 60 ft | Darkness ? Dim (grayscale), Dim ? Bright |
| Superior Darkvision | 120 ft | Darkness ? Dim (grayscale), Dim ? Bright |
| Blindsight | 30 ft | No light needed, detects invisible |
| Tremorsense | 60 ft | Detects vibrations, needs ground contact |
| Truesight | 120 ft | Sees all: darkness, invisible, illusions, ethereal |

## How Darkvision Works
- **In Darkness**: Sees as if it were dim light (shades of gray only)
- **In Dim Light**: Sees as if it were bright light
- **Cannot discern color in darkness**

## How Tremorsense Works
- Detects vibrations within radius
- Must be in contact with same ground/substance
- Cannot detect flying or incorporeal creatures
- Works when blinded

## How Truesight Works
- Sees in normal and magical darkness
- Sees invisible creatures and objects
- Automatically detects visual illusions
- Sees original form of shapechangers
- Can see into Ethereal Plane

## Races with Darkvision
- **60 ft**: Elf, Dwarf, Half-Orc, Tiefling, Gnome, Half-Elf
- **120 ft**: Drow (but has Sunlight Sensitivity)
- **None**: Human, Halfling, Dragonborn

## Conditions & Effects
| Condition | Vision Effect | Combat Effect |
|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| Blinded | Can't see | Disadvantage on attacks |
| Invisible | Hidden | Advantage on attacks |
| Sunlight Sensitivity | - | Disadvantage in bright light |

## Combat Modifiers
| Situation | Modifier |
|-----------|----------|
| Attacker blinded | Disadvantage |
| Target invisible | Disadvantage (attacker) |
| Target blinded | Advantage (attacker) |
| Sunlight sensitivity in bright | Disadvantage |
| Perception in dim light | Disadvantage |
| Perception in darkness (no darkvision) | Auto-fail |

## Tips
1. Elves/Dwarves excel in dungeons (darkvision)
2. Drow powerful at night, weak in daylight
3. Fog Cloud blocks all vision (great for escape)
4. Darkness spell counters light sources
5. Wolves use blindsight (ignore lighting)
